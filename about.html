About

I'm Bryan Barrows — a Senior Technical Account Manager at Okta and a CISSP, working with some of the largest enterprises on their toughest identity problems. My beat for the next decade: identity in the agentic age. What happens to authentication when the "user" is an AI agent acting on someone's behalf, at machine speed and scale?

I learn in public. That means building interactive explainers instead of just reading specs, and writing down what the failures teach. If it can't be explained plainly, it isn't understood yet.

What I believe

  • Authentication should be understandable, not just implementable.
  • The best way to learn a protocol is to break it.
  • Agents don't change identity principles; they scale the consequences.
  • Usable security beats clever security, every time.

How this site is made

Every essay starts from something I built or broke — never from a blank prompt. I use AI for editing, structure, and turning rough notes into drafts. I don't use it for the thinking. The rule: if a post has no scar tissue — no bug I hit, no customer conversation, no thing that surprised me — it doesn't ship.
