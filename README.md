# bbarrows89.github.io

The professional home of Bryan Barrows — *"Identity security, made understandable."*

Live at https://bbarrows89.github.io

## What's here

- **Home** — the thesis, and three proof pillars: what I build, what I write, who I am
- **Essays** — long-form breakdowns of authentication, drawn from real building
- **Playground** — interactive visualizations. Watch it happen, then break it.
- **About** — what I believe, how this site is made, and how to reach me

## How it's built

Hand-written static HTML and CSS — no framework, no build step. Dark mode toggle included (respects your OS preference). Served by GitHub Pages straight from `master`.

## The pipeline

Every other Monday a LinkedIn draft lands; the strong ones get expanded into essays here. LinkedIn is distribution, this site is the permanent home, GitHub is the proof of work.
